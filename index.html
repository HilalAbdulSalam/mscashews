OUR FAMILY STORY · SINCE 1965

One family.
Generations of
cashew know-how.

Our family’s journey in the cashew trade began in 1965, when our great-grandfather laid the foundations of the business in Kollam. What he started has been carried forward by successive generations, each learning from the one before.

Today, M S Cashews continues that family tradition in Kannanalloor. The business remains in family hands, with later generations building on the experience passed down to them.

Kajuva is the next chapter in that story: bringing our family’s experience with cashews to the packs you take home, share and enjoy.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

The little details.

What goes into Kajuva cashews?+

Kajuva contains one ingredient: cashew nuts (100%), drum roasted for rich flavour and crunch. Allergen information: contains nuts.

Which grades and pack sizes can I enquire about?+

Our range includes W180, W240 and W320 whole-cashew grades, with 250 g, 500 g and 1 kg packaging. Contact us to confirm the grade and size combinations currently available.

How should I store my cashews?+

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Once opened, reseal tightly or transfer to an airtight container.

Can I place a bulk or business enquiry?+

Yes. Contact M S Cashews with your preferred grade and quantity. Our team can discuss current availability, pricing and order arrangements with you.
