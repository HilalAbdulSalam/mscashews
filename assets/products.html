CHOOSING YOUR CASHEWS

Product questions.

Which cashew grades can I enquire about?+

Our collection includes W180, W210, W240 and W320 white whole cashews; SW scorched wholes and SS scorched splits; and LWP large white pieces, SWP small white pieces and BB baby bits. Choose a grade above or ask us to help with your requirements.

Which retail pack sizes are available?+

The Kajuva retail collection features W180, W240 and W320 in 250 g, 500 g and 1 kg packs. Please contact us to confirm the current grade and pack-size combinations. Packaging options for other grades are confirmed on enquiry.

How do I enquire about bulk cashew prices?+

Choose your preferred grade and select “Bulk requirement” in the cashew enquiry form. Include the quantity and delivery location in your message. M S Cashews will confirm current prices, availability and order arrangements.

Where is M S Cashews located?+

M S Cashews is based in Thrikkovilvattom, Kannanalloor, Kollam, Kerala, India, postcode 691577. Contact us on +91 96569 15303 or sales@mscashews.com for product and bulk enquiries.
